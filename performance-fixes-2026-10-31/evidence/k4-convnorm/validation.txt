Patch: conv-keynorm.patch, applied after paired-kv-conv.patch.

Arithmetic: four convolution taps retain their fp32 accumulation order and
activation cast before the residual add. K norm consumes the rounded convolution
output, uses native128-wide four-values-per-lane accumulation, precise rsqrt,
and casts the normalized activation before multiplying by the norm weight.
